# Hackathon Topic 🧑‍💻

The hackathon was hosted by Bunq, a Dutch "Neobank" (digital bank). The topic of the hackathon was to build our own AI Agents with the Bunq API and LLMs powered by their partners: AWS and Nvidia. The goal was to create a product that integrated AI Agents, Bunq integrations and LLMs to solve a problem. The hackathon was open to everyone, including students, graduates, and professionals.

# Our Solution 💡

Our *elevator pitch*:
> **"Foxy: Your Financial Planner"** - A personalized financial planner that helps you save money and reach your financial goals. Foxy 🦊is an AI-powered finance autopilot that seamlessly integrates with Bunq, providing you with a personalized financial plan.

With our product, you can talk with Foxy, the AI agent, and you will then together discuss your financial goals, whatever they might be. Think about : 
- buying a house
- saving for a vacation
- saving for a brand new car
- so on...

After that, Foxy 🦊 will create a personalized financial plan for you, based on your current financial situation and your goals. Foxy 🦊 will then help you to reach your goals by providing you with personalized advice and recommendations. Foxy 🦊 will also help you to track your progress.

## Why Foxy? 🔋
One of the most common problems people face with money are long-term investments. Most people don't understand how to save money or how to get started.

That's where Foxy 🦊 helps bunq users by:

- Coaching them through the savings process
- Providing personalized advice based on their interests, income, expenses, and payment history
- Crafting personalized plans that fit perfectly with their lifestyle
Once you and Foxy have agreed on a plan, she will give you additional advice based on your spending habits and will automatically set up a savings account on bunq.

## Features 🚀 
- **AI-Powered Goal Setting**: Tell Foxy your dreams, and she'll help make them financially achievable
- **Smart Budgeting**: AI analysis of your spending patterns creates optimal budget plans
- **Interactive Goal Tracking**: Watch your progress in real-time with engaging visualizations
- **Automated Savings**: Set-and-forget savings that optimize based on your income and expenses
- **Personalized Advice**: Get AI recommendations to reach your goals faster without sacrificing quality of life

## AI Agents : Foxy 🦊

Foxy, is an AI agent that is powered by `nvidia/llama-3.1-nemotron-ultra-253b-v1`. It runs in a python backend server, which has been created with `FastAPI` and uses `OpenAi` to communicate with the LLM.

## Bunq integration 💳

We integrate the bunq api in several places:
- We use the bunq api to get the payments history of the user, so Foxy 🦊 can analyze the spending habits of the user and give personalized advice.
- We also create a savings goal in the application for the user, once Foxy 🦊 has created a financial plan for the user.

## Frontend 🥶

We know that a good presentation and frontend is key to win a hackathon. So we made sure the frontend looks really nice, as well as the user experience. We have used `shadcn/ui` to create a modern and sleek frontend. We have used `React Vite` to create the frontend.

# Conclusion 🏁

I had a great time at the hackathon, I learned a lot, and did not sleep at all 😴 (36+ hrs). I am really happy with the product we made as a team, and proud of what we did in such a short time. We unfortunately did not win any prize, but we went home with a lot of cool merch and having met a lot of new people and some friends.

I am looking forward to the next hackathon!
